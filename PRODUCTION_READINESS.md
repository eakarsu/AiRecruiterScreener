# Governed candidate screening

The supported API is `/api/governance`. It records candidate consent, ATS evidence, job-related criteria, validity, bias/accessibility evaluation, explainable proposals, individualized recruiter and hiring-manager review, interview approval, calendar receipts, candidate feedback, appeal/correction, outcomes, retention, and deletion. It never hires, rejects, ranks on protected traits, sets compensation, schedules, or messages autonomously.

Apply `backend/migrations/001_governed_candidate_screening.sql` separately. Pre-provision employer membership and candidate subject prefixes; public self-provisioning is not a production identity path. ATS, HRIS, LMS, calendar, content, communication, identity, assessment, and lawful background-check workers remain disabled until consent, credentials, typed contracts, sync/deletion propagation, signed receipts, replay protection, retry/dead-letter, and recovery tests pass. The calendar gap is replaced by durable approved scheduling receipts on the supported path.

Use `.env.example`; production rejects weak secrets, wildcard CORS, demo/mock/provider flags, and startup mutation. `start.sh` never installs, seeds, migrates, resets data, or reclaims ports. Employment counsel, accessibility and bias experts, candidates, and independent auditors must validate job relatedness, cohort outcomes, prohibited attributes, accommodations, explanations, appeals, retention/deletion, latency, calendar failures, and realized outcomes before launch. No candidate was screened or employment decision made here.
